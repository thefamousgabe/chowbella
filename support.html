Counting a pot of food

  1. Weigh the empty pot. Save it and Chowbella remembers its weight next time.
  2. Weigh each ingredient raw as it goes in. Skip the water: the last weigh catches it.
  3. Cook, then weigh the full pot.
  4. That’s it. Any scoop on the scale is now counted exactly.

For oil, butter and sauces, weigh the bottle or tub before and after, and Chowbella counts the difference.

Freezing a batch

Once the finished pot is weighed, open the batch and find Freezer. Split it into equal containers, or one meal each for a dog, write each number on its lid, and weigh each container as you fill it. Logging a container marks it eaten. Delete that log entry and it counts as in the freezer again. Labels prints them ten to a page.

Vitamins and supplements

In Settings, under Kitchen, tap Supplements, then New supplement. Type the amounts for one serving as the label prints them, and weigh a serving on the scale. It then shows up when you add food, so it can go into a batch, where the dog check counts it, or into someone’s day. Check the label for xylitol before a human supplement goes in a dog’s food.

On Today, the Vitamins and minerals card shows what each person had as a percent of the FDA Daily Value. For a dog, it marks anything over AAFCO’s maximum.

Common questions

What’s free, and what is Chowbella Plus?

You and one dog are free, with everything in the app. Plus adds everyone else in the house, each with their own targets and log. It comes yearly, with a free week first, or monthly.

I added someone without Plus. What happens to them?

They keep their seat, their card and their whole log, and nothing new is logged for them until Plus is on. Every dog keeps its safety checks, Plus or not.

How do I restore or cancel Plus?

To restore it on a new phone, open Settings in Chowbella and tap Restore purchases. To cancel, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before it renews, or before the free week ends, and you won’t be charged. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does Chowbella work out my dog’s food?

From the dog’s weight, age, whether they are neutered or spayed, and how active they are, using common veterinary starting points. These are estimates. Weigh your dog every couple of weeks and adjust, and if your vet gave you a number, type it in on the dog’s page in Household.

Does the dog check mean a recipe is complete and balanced?

No. The check compares a batch, and any supplement in it, with AAFCO’s published nutrient minimums for dogs and says which ones it meets, which are low and which it can’t tell. Anything added at the bowl isn’t counted. Most homemade dog food needs a supplement, and if a recipe will be your dog’s only food, have your vet see it.

Which foods does Chowbella warn about?

Ingredients that are toxic or risky for dogs, such as onion, garlic, grapes, raisins, chocolate and xylitol, are flagged by name the moment they are added to a dog’s batch. The list can’t cover everything. If your dog ate something that worries you, call your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426 4435. A consultation fee may apply.

The plate scan says it isn’t available.

The plate scan needs an internet connection, and each phone has a daily number of scans. When it can’t read a plate, add the items by hand. Barcodes, labels and typed lists work the same way they always do.

What can I say to Siri?

  • “Feed Bella in Chowbella” logs Bella’s last meal again.
  • “Has Bella eaten in Chowbella” tells you how many meals she’s had today.
  • “How much chili in Chowbella” turns calories into grams of a batch.

Use your own names. “Feed the dog in Chowbella” works too.

Can I use ounces, or kilograms?

Yes. In Settings, Units sets the food on the scale to grams or ounces, and people and dogs to pounds and inches or kilograms and centimeters.

How do I get my data out, or delete it?

In Settings, Your data has Export the log, which gives you every entry as a spreadsheet file, and Erase everything. Everything lives on your phone, so deleting the app removes it too.
