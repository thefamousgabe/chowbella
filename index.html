Chowbella Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026 · Developer: Vileking Studio · Contact: thefilthygarments@gmail.com · Support

The short version

Chowbella keeps your household, your recipes and your food log on your phone. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. Three things can leave your phone, and each one only when you use it: a barcode you scan, a photo of a plate you scan, and a purchase you make through Apple.

What stays on your phone

  • The people and dogs you add: names, weights, heights, birth year or birth date, goals, activity, and any face photo you choose for them.
  • Your kitchen: batches, ingredients, pot weights, freezer containers, supplements, foods you save, and everything you log. Freezer labels are made on your phone and only go where you send or print them.
  • Your settings.
  • Reading labels and lists: nutrition labels, barcodes and typed or dictated ingredient lists are read on your phone, with Apple’s own tools. On phones with Apple Intelligence, tidying up a typed list also happens on the phone.
  • A short record of how the app is used, such as the day you finished setup or which Plus plan you chose. It never holds a name, a weight, a food or a photo, and it is never sent anywhere.

Your food log and your body weights are personal. They are not sent to us, and they are never used for advertising.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Barcodes. When you scan a package, Chowbella sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to look up the product. As with any web request, Open Food Facts can see your internet address.
  • Plate scans. When you scan a plate, the photo is made smaller and sent to Chowbella’s scan server, which runs on Supabase. The server passes it to Anthropic, whose Claude model reads the foods in it, and sends the list back to your phone. The server does not store or log the photo. With the photo, the app sends a random code it made for itself. The code is not your name, your Apple Account or anything about you, and it lets the server cap how many scans one phone makes in a day. The server keeps that code and the count for each day. Nothing else is sent with a scan: no names, weights or log. You check every item before anything is logged.
  • Purchases. Chowbella Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and Chowbella never sees your card or your Apple Account details. The app only learns from Apple, on your phone, whether Plus is on.
  • Siri and Shortcuts. Phrases like “Feed Bella in Chowbella” are handled by Apple and answered by the app on your phone. Apple’s handling of Siri requests is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • No account or sign in.
  • No advertising, and no analytics or crash reporting from other companies.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites. The app’s privacy manifest declares no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, beyond the three uses above.

Your control

  • Export: Settings, then Your data, then Export the log, gives you every entry as a spreadsheet file.
  • Erase: Settings, then Your data, then Erase everything, removes every person, dog, batch, food and log entry from your phone.
  • Delete the app, and its data goes with it.
  • Your phone’s own backups, to iCloud or a computer, include Chowbella’s data as they do for any app. Those backups are between you and Apple.

We hold none of your household or log on a server, so there is nothing of yours for us to look up, hand over or delete there. The scan counts are kept by a random code that isn’t tied to you.

Children

Chowbella is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them. Weight goals are for adults only.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Chowbella sends anything new off your phone, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change to match.
