Numbers are estimates

  • Calories, nutrients and portions come from what you weigh and type, from labels, and from Open Food Facts. Any of them can be off: a label can be wrong, a scale can drift, and a public database can hold mistakes.
  • Calorie targets and weight goals are general estimates for adults. They are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before you make a big change to how you eat, and if you are pregnant, under 18 or managing a health condition.

Feeding your dog

  • Chowbella is not a vet. Its dog portions start from common veterinary estimates, and every dog is different. Weigh your dog every couple of weeks and adjust with your vet.
  • The dog check compares a batch with AAFCO’s published nutrient minimums. Passing it does not mean a recipe is complete and balanced. If homemade food will be your dog’s only food, have your vet or a veterinary nutritionist review the recipe.
  • The list of foods that are risky for dogs can’t cover everything. If your dog ate something that worries you, call your vet or the ASPCA Animal Poison Control Center at (888) 426 4435. A consultation fee may apply.
